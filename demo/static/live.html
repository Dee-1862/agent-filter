              Kept out of your notes

                How does audio become notes?
                1. Your microphone controls who hears you in the call. Notes are a separate choice.
                2. With your notes on, the local meeting host transcribes your speech and holds each line briefly while the local filter checks it.
                3. The local filter keeps sensitive lines out of the final transcript and acts on stop or remove requests.
                4. After everyone finishes, someone can explicitly copy the combined final transcript. The final report shows counts, never withheld words.

                Speech recognition, filtering, and the LiveKit room run on the local host serving this page. No cloud service is involved.
